## Who we are
- East West Homes. Real estate investment, development and property management. HQ Pittsburgh, PA. Founded 2020.
- 57 doors across PA, AL and SC (32 in Pittsburgh). 21 of them are subsidized (voucher). Burgess Court, Sumter SC: a 16-unit flagship. Birmingham metro (Bessemer, Irondale) single-family homes, with owner-finance and rent-to-own paths.
- Six homes under construction, six in preconstruction. We exited Georgia (Savannah).
- Every unit gets a full renovation (roof, HVAC, plumbing, kitchen) whether it rents at market or with a subsidy.

## What we believe
- We house the people most landlords refuse: families leaving shelters, people discharged from care, voucher holders, people coming home from incarceration.
- Same finish standard at every price point.
- We hire minority- and women-owned contractors on purpose.
- Mission and return together: investors expect a real return.
