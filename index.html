We're passionate about creating thriving communities. Rooted in Pittsburgh, we acquire, revitalize, and build homes across the full spectrum of the market. We're not here to cut corners — we invest the extra care and craftsmanship to turn houses into homes people are proud to live in.

Affordable, high-quality housing for underserved communities has always been our foundation, and we partner with the Urban Redevelopment Authority and the Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher program to expand access to it. Today our work reaches from thoughtful restorations to premium homes and new construction from the ground up — but our mission never changes: quality homes, stronger neighborhoods, and lasting value for the families and investors we serve.

Who we're for

At the end of the day, this is about people. It's about the single mother who needs a safe home for her kids. It's about families and first-time buyers who've been overlooked by everyone else. Most of the industry chases the same comfortable buyer — we're here for the people society too often forgets.

That's why we get creative with owner financing and rent-to-own paths, like the families becoming homeowners through our work in Birmingham. And it's not just who we build for — it's who we build with. We intentionally hire minority- and women-owned contractors and subcontractors, the skilled people who never got their shot.

We believe everyone deserves an opportunity — a home, a livelihood, a stake in their community — and that belief drives everything we do.

Everyone deserves a place to call home — and a fair shot at building something of their own.

We're a Pittsburgh-based real estate investment and development company, but our roots run deeper than any single market. We started by taking on the properties others walked away from — distressed, forgotten houses — and revitalizing them into safe, quality, affordable homes.

Over time our work has grown to span the full spectrum, from careful restorations to premium homes and ground-up new construction. Through all of it, one thing has never changed: we do the work right, and we do it for the people who need it most.

Leadership

Mervet Taha

Co-Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Mervet built the machine this company runs on. She came to real estate after two decades in software, where she led enterprise sales for a series of technology companies, and she brought that discipline with her — the systems, the reporting, the operational rigor that let a single house in Birmingham become a portfolio across three states.

She founded East West Homes in 2020 and today oversees all operations and drives the strategic and management decisions across the organization. A cum laude graduate of the University of Massachusetts, she is the reason the company can grow without losing its footing.

Michael Head

Co-Founder & President

Michael runs construction, acquisitions, and technology. A University of Pittsburgh graduate and former Pitt athlete, he entered real estate in 2003 — first as a loan officer, then in distressed paper, and through 2008 selling homes as a realtor and renovating property in California. From 2008 to 2012 he consulted full time, with a particular focus on helping formerly incarcerated people build real estate portfolios of their own. He still mentors today.

He returned to the business in 2020 and has been building ever since. That work is personal: Michael was formerly incarcerated himself, and it is the reason this company builds for people the industry writes off.

What we do

We work across the full spectrum of residential real estate — always with the same standard of care, whatever the price point.

How we buy

We source both on- and off-market deals, hunting for real value others miss. That discipline is what lets us stay competitive and financially sound while still delivering quality homes at prices our communities can afford. Buying right is what makes the rest of it possible.

Affordable & distressed revitalization

This is our foundation. We take on the properties others overlook and restore them into safe, quality homes — partnering with the Urban Redevelopment Authority and the Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher program to keep quality housing within reach for the families who need it most.

Premium homes

We bring that same craftsmanship to higher-end renovations — investing the extra care to deliver homes people are proud to own, not corners cut for a quick sale.

Ground-up construction

In Pittsburgh, we're building new homes from the ground up — creating value and adding quality housing stock to the neighborhoods we serve.

Building opportunity

How we build matters as much as what we build. We intentionally partner with underserved and disenfranchised contractors — skilled tradespeople who too often get passed over for the work they've earned. For many, our projects have meant steady income and a way to provide for their families when other doors stayed closed. When we invest in a neighborhood, we invest in the people who live and work there too.

From a family's first home to a brand-new build, every project reflects the same belief: do the work right, and build something that lasts.

Communities & partnerships

Great housing doesn't happen in isolation. We work hand in hand with the organizations and programs that make quality, affordable homes possible — and we get creative to open doors that would otherwise stay shut.

Who we work with

These are working relationships, not logos on a page. Each one puts a real family in a real home.

Housing Authority of the City of Pittsburgh

We are an approved Housing Choice Voucher landlord. HACP inspects our units and pays housing assistance directly each month.

Allegheny County — landlord program

We take placements through the county’s landlord program, housing voucher holders across the county including Homestead.

UPMC

We house tenants referred through UPMC care teams, working directly with their case managers and accepting UPMC-paid rent — housing tied to continuity of care.

Sumter Housing Authority

Voucher-supported households across our 16-unit Burgess Court community in South Carolina.

Pittsburgh Land Bank — Urban Redevelopment Authority

A repeat developer with the Land Bank, cleared through its vetting on every acquisition and working across Spring Hill, Garfield, and the East End. The Land Bank exists to return vacant and tax-delinquent property to productive use. That’s not a box we check — it’s the work itself.

Renting shouldn't be the end of the road

Through owner financing and rent-to-own paths, we help families who've been shut out of traditional lending become homeowners — building equity, stability, and something to pass on. It's already happening: families in our Birmingham market are on the path to owning the homes they live in.

When neighborhoods thrive, everyone benefits. That's the future we're building — one home, one family, one community at a time.

Work with us

We're always looking for skilled, reliable contractors and subcontractors to grow with us. We believe in giving opportunities to the people who've earned them — and in building lasting relationships with the trades who help us do great work.

We're especially committed to partnering with minority- and women-owned businesses and contractors who've been overlooked elsewhere. If you do quality work and take pride in it, we want to hear from you.

Bidding takes about three minutes. We ask for your number, two references, and a couple of photos of past work. Licensing, insurance, and tax paperwork come later — only once we've selected you.
